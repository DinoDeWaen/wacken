# Wacken Planner 2026 V2.17

## Scope

V2.17 is a signed local release APK for the group-rating placement cleanup.

Changes:

- Band overview rows no longer show per-person group ratings under the band
  name.
- Band detail keeps group ratings directly under the user's own rating controls.
- Schedule decision detail candidates now include compact per-person group
  ratings under the candidate's main rating line.
- README and business requirements now describe the updated rating placement.
